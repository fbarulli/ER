Systematic flags among the 175 replay approvals: 26 have a record with multiple package-material values, ten have multiple package formats, eight have an organic declaration on one side, and six have different populated stored mode_type values. Six low-scoring approval pairs share a long normalized title somewhere in their source rows; the title overlap is not proof of matching retail-pack identity. These flags use stored feature values and identify review candidates, not proven mistakes.

Description provenance: source configuration maps description_short_eng to description. Of 2557 nonempty saved descriptions, 1741 are 190–210 characters; the maximum is 235. The audit included the entire saved short field, not entire retailer-page descriptions. The earlier full-description wording overstated source completeness.

Recurring design issue: a bag of familiar words loses selected SKU structure. Lists of choices, multiword flavors, cultivar modifiers, functional subtypes and retail pack levels require explicit source context. Adding more dictionary words alone will not separate these cases.

Review policy must also retain source uncertainty. Organic/caffeine markers on only one side do not prove the opposite on the other. Multiple packaging values can indicate conflicting extraction or nested packaging; provenance and level matter. Source-supported glass/plastic rejection should not be overridden because names look alike.

Priority order: selected-variant parsing; leading/set-of/description-only pack evidence; formulation/type distinctions; packaging conflict review and data-source completeness. Detailed listings and fields are preserved in MORE_PATTERNS_7.json.
