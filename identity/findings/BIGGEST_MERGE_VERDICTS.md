carries the machine-readable detail). Method: rows are grouped by **normalized feed**
(`normalize_retailer`; a feed with country domains or casing variants — e.g. Vitalabo
×8 domain rows, Co-op ×44 rows — speaks ONCE). Each feed is represented by its most
complete row; dissent is decided with the production predicates themselves

manufacture a majority nor hide a conflict.

## 3. Caveats

- **Pack sentinel**: `pack {1.0}` is the explicit "not observed" sentinel, kept in the
  JSON `value_counts` but excluded from conflicts. Pack columns like `{1.0}:9, {6.0}:1`
  are NOT pack disagreements — the `{6.0}` row is the only speaker (e.g. Smart Juice
  00894357002585 Target claims a 6-pack under the single-item GTIN).
- **One-sided bundle listings**: Amazon rows encode retail bundles without a
  counterpart ("16 pack - 16oz. cans" in A SHOC description; Harmless 32oz "(6 Pack)";
  Bev Energy 12-pack; Tres Agaves 2-pack). The census can only catch these when BOTH
  sides populate pack; when only the bundle side speaks there is no predicate
  evidence. 6 such signals found; kept as informational, no holds (absence of pack on
  the sibling is not evidence of scope mismatch).
- **Self-inconsistent feeds exist**: Vitalabo's 21 Cosmeda rows (04032108169224, held
  out of the doc since a single-feed family makes no merge claim) disagree with each
  other on flavor across country domains — evidence that per-feed validation is worth
  a pass later.
- Cosmetic/compatible mosaics (flavor supersets like `coffee` vs `coffee, latte`,
  brand subsets `jones` vs `jones, soda`, volume 1750 vs 1751 within tolerance) are
  CLEAN — the predicates already refuse to mint conflicts from them.
